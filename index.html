Inside MITHRA

Every release has doors you never checked.

MITHRA walks through all of them. It attacks, clicks, fuzzes and load-tests your product the way a hacker, a tester and a real user would, then tells you exactly what broke.

What it audits

Six ways in. One report.

01

Security scans

Finds vulnerabilities across your pages and maps them by severity, so you know what to fix first.

02

API authorization

Checks whether one user can reach another user's data, the bug scanners most often miss.

03

Logged-in scans

Signs in like a real user and audits everything behind the login, not just the public front door.

04

Browser testing

Drives a real browser through your flows: clicks, forms, navigation, and what breaks along the way.

05

Fuzz & load testing

Throws malformed input at every field and real traffic at every endpoint to see what gives first.

06

AI test cases

Reads your app and source files and writes the test cases for you, ready to run and export as a report.

How it works

Point. Audit. Fix.

01

Point it at your app

A URL, a login, or your source files. That's the whole setup.

02

MITHRA goes through every door

Scans, attacks, fuzzes, load-tests and clicks through your product, all at once.

03

Get the report

Every finding ranked by severity, with what broke and where, ready to hand to your team.

Runs on-prem

Your code never leaves your machine. MITHRA runs as a desktop app on your own computer, with a local AI model if you want one.
